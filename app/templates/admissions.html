Admission Process

1
Submit Inquiry

Fill out the admission inquiry form with student and parent details.

2
Document Verification

Submit required documents: birth certificate, previous report card, and address proof.

3
Interaction / Assessment

Students may be invited for a brief interaction or age-appropriate assessment.

4
Confirmation

Upon selection, complete fee payment and receive the admission confirmation letter.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nursery: Child must be 3+ years as on 31st March
  • LKG to Class X: Based on previous class completion and age criteria
  • Transfer cases: TC and mark sheet from previous school required
